# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Party: your location goes out at most every 5 ticks while you move (was 2), gear at most every 10 ticks and only when it changed (run and special energy alone no longer count), and at most 4 drops per kill, valued from 1,000 gp. This keeps the load on RuneLite's party server low.
- *Hop* refuses PvP, high-risk, Deadman, seasonal, beta and similar worlds, and members worlds for free players, and says why.
- *World map orb opens this map* is now off by default; the orb entry only reorders the menu and never hides a game option. Open the map from the sidebar or a hotkey.
- The *Rejoin party* button over the game takes the whole click, so the game never sees half of it.
- *Write routes.log* (Route settings, off by default): routes.log is only written when you turn it on.
- No more chat message about the route planner for Shortest Path users.
- An icon's card no longer loads the opening sentences of its wiki page (and the setting *Load wiki summaries* is gone); *Wiki* still opens the page.
- The next-step text over your character shows outside instances and in a house only, not in boss or raid instances.
- The whole-map download pauses briefly between tiles, tries a failing tile at most three times and then skips it, and no longer re-downloads what the disk limit deleted; the disk limit is kept after each download too.
- Fixed: routing could stop for good after an out-of-memory error during a search ("Searching…" until a restart); *Fastest order* could lose stops added while it ran or leave its button disabled; two routes planned at once could mix up their settings; a restart of the plugin could keep an old custom route running.
- Fixed: a doubled icon where the map draws a place elsewhere (Kalphite Lair, the Dagannoth Kings); the map repainting non-stop while a party member's XP or loot drops were animating; key handlers left behind after switching *Map inside the game window*.
- Defaults: icon size 18, route tile fill 75 and border 100, a brighter yellow route.
- Faster: the route search allocates far less, the map draws its icons with less work per frame, the search index is built in the background, and bundled tables are read once.
- Development: one-off debug tools removed from the test sources and the tools; `update-map-data.sh` also rebuilds `world_map_moves.tsv` and `npc_no_location.tsv`.
- The map over the game is now part of RuneLite's own window by default, over the game view, instead of a window of its own: the desktop sees one window, so the taskbar and alt-tab behave as usual. *Map inside the game window* (off) brings back the separate window.
- Route tiles you pass fade away at once and quicker (0.6 s), right behind you instead of many tiles back.
- Routes to the Kalphite Queen go down the Kalphite Lair's tunnel entrance (with a rope) into her chamber; they no longer go through the Ruins of Uzer, whose basement was wrongly guessed to lie above her chamber. Routes through the Shantay Pass take the pass instead of flying round it by glider and fairy ring.
- The map over the game never takes the keyboard (only while you type in its search field or a route's name), and is a helper window of the game's for the desktop: the desktop keeps the game as the active window, so clicking RuneLite in the taskbar brings it back while the map is open (seen on KDE Plasma, Wayland).
- Routes to Nex: through the frozen door on the God Wars Dungeon's top floor and the Ancient Prison's corridor to her door.
- Many places routes could not get to now can: 115 doors, stiles, gaps, webs and cave entrances on the edge of ground no route reached, found from the game's objects by a new audit (among them the way to Sarachnis, Cam Torum's passages and fenced hunter areas). The places routes cannot reach went from 190 to 158.
- The place search goes word by word, in any order, and forgives a small typo: "shark catherby" suggests *Shark fishing spots – Catherby* (click it for that spot, with every other one marked), "bank varrock" Varrock's bank, "draynr" Draynor Village. Switching the search between places, monsters and items shows the suggestions for what you typed at once.
- Right-clicking an icon shows its details in the card as well as the menu: a dungeon's details without going in.
- Places the wiki map has not drawn (the Kalphite Queen's chamber, a new cave such as the Stalker Den) show a floor plan from the route data, floor and walls, instead of black; checked a region at a time, so they show beside the drawn ones.
- Going into a dungeon (clicking its entrance, or walking in while the map follows you) fits the map to that dungeon, its walkable area from the route data, instead of every dungeon its wiki map holds.
- *Routes* and *Friends* are as large as the floor buttons and stacked right above them; custom routes open beside the Routes button over the game. *Friends* shows as soon as you are in a party, with every member (grey when not sharing where they are).
- Two buttons at the bottom left of the map, above the floor buttons: *Routes* (custom routes; no longer beside the search field) and *Friends*, which now lists only your party (click someone to see where they are and what they carry); the game's friends list is no longer shown or read.
- Bosses fought in an instance mark the way into their lair, not just the area: the Pool of Nightmares for the Nightmare and Phosani's Nightmare, the Royal Titans' tunnel in the Asgarnian Ice Dungeon, the Slayer Tower's roof entrance for the Grotesque Guardians, Zulrah's boat, the Hespori patch, Skotizo's altar, Nex's door, the Doom of Mokhaiotl's lobby, Amoxliatl's door and each Barrows brother's mound (`data/boss_entrances.tsv`).
- *Your house* is a section of the plugin's settings (jewellery box, mounted glory, fairy ring, spirit tree, portals) instead of a right-click window; being in your own house fills it in, and what was saved before is taken over.
- Rowboats with several stops (Lake Molch, Custodia Pass, Tal Teklan and Kastori) show where they go and are used by routes: each boat names the other stops, so the stop it is at is the one it does not name. Fossil Island's rowboats still have no lines (their stops cannot be told apart from the names alone).

- Faster map: icons, place names, search arrows and labels are drawn on whole pixels from pictures made once (drawing them on fractions of a pixel made Java resample each one every frame); the game's icons are only looked at near the view. A full-screen frame far out took about 12 ms, now about 4; hovering the map costs next to nothing.

Click a monster in *Dropped by* to see where it is, *Back* returns to the item.
